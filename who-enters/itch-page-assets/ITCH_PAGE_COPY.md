## Paste-ready description

### Who Enters?

The castle gate opens for no one without your verdict.

In this mobile-first, portrait strategy game, you are the keeper of a troubled gate. Read each visitor's documents and visible clues, then make the call: **swipe right to Admit** or **swipe left to Deny**.

Survive **5 nights × 8 visitors**: 40 decisions in all. You begin with **5 integrity seals**; an incorrect official verdict breaks one. Your choices feed branching, recurring stories that return across the nights, and can lead to **four endings**.

Who will you trust? What will your mercy cost? And who truly enters?

### How to play

- Read the decree, visitor card, documents, and visible cues.
- Swipe right to **Admit**.
- Swipe left to **Deny**.
- Keyboard fallback: **Right** / **D** admits; **Left** / **A** denies.
- Protect your five integrity seals while following the branching stories.
